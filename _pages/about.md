Welcome! I am a third-year PhD student in Business Economics at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://www.anderson.ucla.edu/degrees/phd-program/areas-of-study/global-economics-and-management/meet-the-students/rafael-macedo-rubi%C3%A3o), in the Global Economics and Management area at the Anderson School of Management.

My research interests lie in the fields of behavioral, development, and political economy, especially in questions related to how norms and beliefs interact with institutions. I am also broadly interested in questions related to preferences formation and discrimination.

Before UCLA, I worked for the [World Bank](https://www.worldbank.org) and the [Inter-American Development Bank (IDB)](https://www.iadb.org/en/about-us/departments/res). I hold a master's degree in Economics with Distinction from [University College London (UCL)](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/economics) and an undergraduate degree in Economics from the [Federal University of Minas Gerais (UFMG)](https://www.face.ufmg.br/departamentos/ciencias-economicas/o-departamento.html), in Brazil.

You can find my papers on the [research page](/research/), the tools I share on the [code & resources page](/code/), and a PDF of my [CV here](/cv/).

In my free time I like playing Brazilian music with friends, reading literature, and enjoying the nature.
