Welcome! I am a PhD student at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://www.anderson.ucla.edu/degrees/phd-program/areas-of-study/global-economics-and-management/meet-the-students/rafael-macedo-rubi%C3%A3o). I am part of the Global Economics and Management Department, at the Anderson School of Management. Previously, I worked for the [World Bank](https://www.worldbank.org) and the [Inter-American Development Bank (IDB)](https://www.iadb.org/en/about-us/departments/res). I have a master's degree in economics with Distinction from the [University College London (UCL)](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/economics), and an undergraduate degree in Economics from the [Federal University of Minas Gerais (UFMG)](https://www.face.ufmg.br/departamentos/ciencias-economicas/o-departamento.html). 

My research interests lie in the fields of behavioral, development, and political economy, especially in questions related to how norms and beliefs interact with institutions. I am also broadly interested in questions related to preferences formation and discrimination. 

In my most recent working paper, joint with Ricardo Perez-Truglia, we ask whether people overestimate how much income raises life satisfaction — and whether correcting that belief changes the choices they make. [*Is Money Overrated? Misperceived Satisfaction from Income*](https://www.nber.org/papers/w35423) is out as NBER Working Paper No. 35423.

In a second working paper, with Juan Pablo Chauvin and Miguel Talamas (RES-IDB), we measure how badly births to child mothers are undercounted around the world, and why. [*The Undercounting of Births to Child Mothers*](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6153086) is available on SSRN.

I have two further projects underway: one, also with Juan Pablo Chauvin and Miguel Talamas, estimating the effects of changes in the minimum legal age of sexual consent in Mexican States on outcomes such as early fertility, early marriage, and crime incidence; and another, joint with Julian Martinez-Correa (UChicago), estimating how much negative labor market experiences affect people's political leaning and voting behavior, and the reasons behind it.

You can find all of it, with abstracts and links, on my [research page](/research/). I also share the tools I build for my own work — survey instruments, replication code, and agent skills — on the [code and resources page](/code/).

For my MSc dissertation (available upon request), I investigated the effects of the 4G mobile internet roll-out on mental health in the UK.

In my free time I like playing Brazilian music with friends, reading literature, and enjoying the nature.
