What the five games mean

Simultaneous Bertrand
Both firms set their prices at the same time, as in the Bertrand model from class, so Airbus cannot see your price. It charges its Bertrand–Nash equilibrium price in every round. Find the price that maximizes your profit against it.
Sequential take turns
You and Airbus take turns, starting from the cartel price. You see Airbus’s current price and choose yours; this round’s profits use both prices. Airbus then switches to its best response to your price, which you see before your next move. If both firms keep best-responding, prices converge to the Bertrand–Nash equilibrium.
Leader Stackelberg: you move first
You set your price first. Airbus sees it and replies with its best response, so choose your price anticipating that reaction.
Follower Stackelberg: Airbus moves first
Airbus commits to its price first (you see it before you choose), anticipating your best response. Your job is to find the best response to Airbus’s price.
Collusion cartel
Airbus matches whatever price you set, as if the two firms had agreed to charge the same price. With identical firms, the price that maximizes your profit then also maximizes the two firms’ joint profit.
