US minimum wages, nominal and price-adjusted

Minimum wages in effect on October 24, 2026, shown side by side in dollars and adjusted for local price levels (BEA Regional Price Parities, 2024). Each page lets you hover or search for a place and see its nominal wage, price level and real wage.

By state →Statewide rates for the 50 states and DC, with a sortable table of how ranks change after the price adjustment. By county →3,144 counties, including regional tiers and city and county ordinances (population-weighted), with metro or nonmetro price levels.

Sources: US Department of Labor, state and city labor offices, BEA Regional Price Parities, Census population estimates. Data choices are listed at the bottom of each page. Prepared by Rafael Macedo-Rubião.
